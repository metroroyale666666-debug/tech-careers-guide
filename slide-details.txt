# Slide Details for Technical Professions Presentation

## Slide 1: Introduction to Technical Professions
- **Content:** Overview of the presentation and what to expect.
- **Elements:** Title slide, images of various professions.
- **Time Allocation:** 2 minutes.
- **Presentation Recommendations:** Start with a hook; ask students what technical professions they know.

## Slide 2: What Are Technical Professions?
- **Content:** Define technical professions and their importance in today's world.
- **Elements:** Definitions, graphics of tech tools.
- **Time Allocation:** 3 minutes.
- **Presentation Recommendations:** Use examples from daily life.
